Privacy Policy

Field Call Works · Effective October 3, 2026 · Last updated October 3, 2026

Field Call Works ("Field Call Works," "we," "us") provides an AI-powered phone receptionist for home-service businesses, such as roofing companies. When you call one of these businesses, our virtual receptionist may answer, collect your details, and pass them to the business so they can call you back. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

If you are a caller, the business you called (our "Client") decides how it uses your information after we deliver it. Please see that business's own privacy policy for its practices.

1. Information we collect

When you call

  • Call recording and transcript. Calls answered by our virtual receptionist are recorded and transcribed. You are told this at the start of the call.
  • Caller ID. The phone number you call from, and the time and length of the call.
  • Details you give us. Your name, callback phone number, property address, a description of the issue, and your preferred callback time.
  • Text-message consent. Whether you agreed to receive text messages.
  • Call analysis. An automatically generated summary, topics, and sentiment of the call.

When you book an appointment

If the receptionist schedules an appointment, we may also collect your first and last name and email address to create a calendar event.

From our Clients

Business contact details for the Client and the people who receive lead notifications.

2. How we use information

  • To answer calls, understand your request, and respond to you.
  • To send your request to the business you called so they can follow up.
  • To send you a text confirmation, only if you agreed to receive texts.
  • To schedule, change, or cancel appointments you request.
  • To review call quality, fix problems, and improve the service.
  • To prevent spam, fraud, and misuse, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

3. Text messaging (SMS)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except the service providers that deliver the messages for us.

  • We text you only if you agree during the call. Agreeing is not required to get service.
  • Messages are about your request: confirmations and appointment updates. Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction, deletion. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • Text messages. Reply STOP at any time.
  • Recording. If you do not want to be recorded, you can hang up and contact the business another way.

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other U.S. states with privacy laws), you may have additional rights. We will not discriminate against you for using them. To make a request, contact us below. We may need to verify your identity, usually by confirming the phone number you called from. If your request is about a business you called, we may forward it to them or ask you to contact them directly.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information, and we rely on providers that maintain their own security programs. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Our service is meant for adults arranging home services. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child gave us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will take reasonable steps to give notice.
